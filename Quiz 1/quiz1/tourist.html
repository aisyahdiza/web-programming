A Guide to Enjoy South Tangerang

If you're visiting South Tangerang, here's how to spend your time well, from modern hangouts to a taste of local culture.

  • BSD City

    The heart of modern South Tangerang. Malls, cafes, and entertainment spots make it perfect for a full day out, whether you're shopping, working from a coffee shop, or just people watching.

  • Setu Babakan

    A short trip away, this is where you can experience traditional Betawi culture up close, from local food to cultural performances. A nice contrast to the city's modern side.

  • Taman Kota 2 BSD

    A spacious green park, great for jogging, picnics, or just slowing down for a bit. Proof that South Tangerang still holds onto its green spaces amid all the development.

  • Culinary Hunting

    No visit is complete without trying the local flavors. Head to Pasar Modern BSD or the warungs around Setu Babakan to taste dishes like Laksa Tangerang, Sayur Besan, or a warm glass of Bir Pletok, the kind of food you won't easily find elsewhere.
