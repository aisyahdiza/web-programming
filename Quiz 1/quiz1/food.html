4 Unique Foods You Must Try in South Tangerang

Exploring South Tangerang isn't complete without a taste of its local flavors! Here are 4 unique dishes handpicked by a local that you simply must try when visiting the city.

  1. Laksa Tangerang

    A rich, savory noodle soup with a distinct coconut-based broth, different from other regional laksa variants.

  2. Sayur Besan

    A traditional dish typically served during weddings, combining sweet potatoes, petai, and coconut milk in a unique sweet-savory blend.

  3. Gecom (Toge Oncom)

    A simple yet flavorful street food made from bean sprouts and fermented oncom, stir-fried with spices.

  4. Bonus: Bir Pletok

    Not actual alcohol, this is a traditional Betawi herbal drink made from ginger and spices, perfect for warming up.
