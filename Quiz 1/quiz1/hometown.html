Fun Facts About South Tangerang

South Tangerang is a city in Banten Province, sitting right on the southwestern edge of Jakarta. It's close enough to feel the capital's energy, but with a character all its own.

The name comes from its position as the southern part of the greater Tangerang area, officially becoming its own city in 2008. Since then, it's grown into one of Indonesia's fastest developing urban areas. It's often nicknamed a "satellite city" for Jakarta, and increasingly known as a hub for education, home to universities like UIN Syarif Hidayatullah Jakarta, Universitas Pembangunan Jaya (UPJ), and Universitas Pamulang (UNPAM), among others.

Fun Facts:

  • Born as its own city back in 2008, breaking away from Tangerang Regency to chart its own path.
  • Home to several well-known universities, making it a hub for students and young professionals.
  • Known for its rapid development of modern housing complexes and shopping centers like BSD City and Alam Sutera.
